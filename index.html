What is the GI Badge?

GI

GI stands for Geographical Indication. It is a tag given to products that come from a particular region and have special quality because of that place. A product with the GI badge is verified to be made in its original region by traditional methods. Products without a GI tag carry a Handmade Heritage tag instead.
